Independent scientific assurance

AI-generated does not automatically mean scientifically reliable.

Correct terminology and polished language are not sufficient evidence of scientific quality. Important errors can arise in endpoint selection, statistical assumptions, estimand strategy, analysis methods, or dependencies between decisions.

We evaluate these scientific dimensions with documented scope, evidence, and limitations so organizations can understand what has been assessed and where risk remains.

Scientific appropriateness

Assess whether statistical decisions make sense for the indication, design, and intended clinical question.

Consistency across dependent elements

Check whether endpoints, estimands, analysis methods, assumptions, and related decisions align.

Evidence-based findings

Report observed strengths, failures, assessment boundaries, and actionable recommendations.

Independent assurance engagements are scoped separately from work in which AIGxP Labs helped create or refine the assessed content. Our role and potential conflicts are disclosed.
